I’m Ziyan Shi, a full-stack engineer at Blux and a PhD student in the joint program between Westlake University and the Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation (SMART). I build research tools for animal behavior tracking and brain–computer interfaces, with research interests in whole-brain dynamics, sleep, consciousness, and memory.
